The [2026 edition](https://ansperformance.eu/study/data-challenge/dc2026/) of
the PRC Data Challenge follows in the footsteps of the
[2024](https://ansperformance.eu/study/data-challenge/dc2024/) and
[2025](https://ansperformance.eu/study/data-challenge/dc2025/) editions and
proposes to build Machine Learning (ML) models to predict the
the taxi-out time of flights departing from 10 major European airports.  
The training dataset consists of of all movements of the 10 airports
 for the full year of 2025.
